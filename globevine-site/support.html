Support

We're happy to help.

Contact us

Email info@globevineapp.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. It helps if you tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and the version of Globevine you're using (shown on the App Store page under "Version History").

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Globevine works without an account or sign-in.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Your name, date of birth and progress are never sent to us. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I got a new iPhone. Will my progress move over?

Globevine doesn't sync between devices. If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your Globevine progress is usually restored along with your other apps.

How do I start over?

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Reinstall it from the App Store to start fresh. Note that this can't be undone.

I found a mistake in the wine content.

Thank you! Please email us with the region, grape or winemaker and what needs correcting, and we'll fix it in a future update.
