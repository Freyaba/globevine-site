Support

We usually reply within a few working days.

Email us at info@globevineapp.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened. Please do not send your date of birth or other personal details unless we ask.

Common questions

How do I delete my data? If you have an account, open Settings in the app and choose Delete my account. Your profile, progress and sign-in details are deleted straight away. You can also email us. Journal notes and photos are only on your device, so deleting the app removes them. Without an account, deleting the app deletes everything and we hold no copy.

Can I move my progress to a new phone? Yes, if you have an account: sign in on the new phone and your progress syncs. Without an account, progress stays on the device, although restoring an iPhone backup may bring it across. Journal notes and photos are not synced.

I entered my date of birth wrongly. The app does not keep your date of birth. Delete the app and reinstall it to set up a new profile (with an account, delete the account first or email us). If you were refused because you are under the age limit, the app has not saved anything.

I think a fact in the app is wrong. Please tell us which question, region, grape or producer, and what you think is correct. Wine facts change, and we want to get them right.

Does Globevine collect my information? Only if you create an account, and then only what the privacy policy lists. Without an account, nothing leaves your device.

Drinking responsibly

Globevine is a learning game for adults. If you drink, please do so responsibly. Advice and support are available from Drinkaware.
