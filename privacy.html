Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how the Globevine app ("Globevine", "we", "us") handles your information. In short: Globevine does not collect, store or share your personal data on any server. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

When you use Globevine, the app may ask for your name and date of birth and will keep track of your progress in the app. This information:

  • is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app;
  • is never sent to us or to any third party;
  • is used only within the app, for example to personalise your experience;
  • is permanently deleted when you delete the app.

Globevine has no user accounts and does not sync data between devices. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or your computer, app data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Information we do not collect

Globevine does not use analytics or advertising tools, does not track you across apps or websites, does not access your location, contacts or photos, and does not sell or share any data.

App Store and Apple diagnostics

Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated information such as download numbers, and crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). This information does not identify you personally. You can turn this sharing on or off at any time in your iPhone settings.

Age

Globevine is about wine and is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are always in control: you can change or remove your information in the app, or delete it entirely by deleting the app. If you have any questions about your rights under data protection law (including the UK GDPR), please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Globevine handles information, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@globevineapp.com.
